FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

NEED QUICK ANSWERS?

Where are items shipped from?

All items are hand-curated, steam-sanitized, and dispatched directly from our flagship atelier in Bettiah, West Champaran, Bihar across India.

How do I know the exact sizing?

Since vintage sizes vary by decade, we provide both tagged size and exact pit-to-pit (inches) & length measurements on every product card.

What is the return policy?

Because vintage garments are 1-of-1, sales are generally final. However, if there is an unstated defect, we offer 100% store credit exchange within 48 hours of delivery.
